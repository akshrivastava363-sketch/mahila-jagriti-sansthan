Our Founding Story

How one woman's courage became a movement

Mahila Jagriti Sansthan (MJS) was not born out of an idea — it emerged from lived realities.

In the early 1990s, across the villages of Darbhanga, Bihar, countless women were silently enduring lives shaped by violence, lack of education, and deep financial dependency. There were no support systems to turn to, and children were often pushed into labour, deprived of both childhood and opportunity. Dignity and growth were distant aspirations for many.

It was in this environment that Mrs. Indira Kumari, deeply moved by what she witnessed around her, began working at the grassroots level. She started by simply listening — to women's stories, their struggles, and their resilience. Gradually, she began helping them access their rights and mobilising communities to stand together. What began as small, compassionate interventions soon revealed a much larger need for sustained and structured support.

In 1993, with limited resources but unwavering determination, she founded Mahila Jagriti Sansthan with a clear vision — to build a society where women and children can live with dignity, safety, and equal opportunity.

The journey was far from easy. There was no funding, minimal infrastructure, and significant social resistance. Yet, what sustained MJS was the trust of the community and a relentless commitment to bring change where it was needed the most.

Over time, MJS evolved into a strong grassroots movement, impacting thousands of lives. It has empowered over 15,000 women through skill development, awareness, and livelihood support, and has rescued and rehabilitated more than 3,500 children from child labour, trafficking, and exploitation.

The organisation has worked closely with institutions, government bodies, and communities to strengthen protection systems, while also addressing cases of gender-based violence at the ground level — often becoming the first point of support for survivors.

Today, MJS stands as a bridge between vulnerability and opportunity, not only providing immediate support but also creating pathways for long-term independence and dignity.

And yet, the journey continues. Every day brings new stories, new challenges, and new individuals seeking hope and protection.

At its core, MJS is driven by a simple belief — that real change begins at the grassroots, and it begins with courage.

Our Vision & Mission

The principles that guide everything we do

🌟 Our Vision

To build a just, inclusive, and equitable society where every woman and child lives with dignity, safety, and equal opportunity — free from violence, discrimination, and exploitation.

🎯 Our Mission

Mahila Jagriti Sansthan (MJS) is committed to working at the grassroots to empower women, protect children, and strengthen communities through sustainable and inclusive interventions.

Our mission is to create lasting change by promoting women's economic independence, ensuring access to education and protection for children, and addressing gender-based violence through prevention, support, and rehabilitation.

We strive to provide safe spaces, legal and psychosocial support, and livelihood opportunities to women in crisis, while building awareness and community systems that challenge inequality and violence.

Through continuous engagement with communities, institutions, and stakeholders, MJS works to transform vulnerability into strength and create pathways for dignity, justice, and self-reliance.
